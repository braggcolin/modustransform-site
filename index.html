Services

Four ways I help change portfolios deliver.

PMO Setup

A PMO sized to your organisation, not a textbook. Governance, stage gates, RAID, reporting cadence and financial control, built so delivery teams use it rather than work around it.

PMO Modernisation

Replace spreadsheet chasing with self-serve reporting. Power BI, Power Automate, PPM tooling and practical AI that cut admin and put live portfolio data in front of decision makers.

Delivery Recovery

For portfolios reported green that are actually off track. Audit every project, re-baseline budgets and dates, rebuild the team and restore stakeholder confidence with a plan that holds.

Complex Change and Transformation

Carve-outs, TSA exits, application separation and large-scale migrations. Cross-portfolio sequencing where workstreams compete for the same people and infrastructure.
